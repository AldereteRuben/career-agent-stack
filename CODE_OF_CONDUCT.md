# Community conduct / Convivencia

Be respectful, patient and specific. Welcome contributors of different backgrounds, languages and experience levels. Critique the work with evidence and propose improvements.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexual harassment, spam and publishing another person's private information are not acceptable. Do not use issues or PRs to promote unauthorized commercial copies.

The maintainer, [@AldereteRuben](https://github.com/AldereteRuben), may edit or remove inappropriate content, close disruptive discussions, or restrict participation. Responses should be proportionate to the behavior. Report abusive content using GitHub's reporting controls; do not repost private information as evidence in a public issue. A dedicated confidential community contact is not yet available.

## Español

Participa con respeto y paciencia. Critica las propuestas, no a las personas. No se permite acoso, discriminación, amenazas, spam ni divulgar información privada. El responsable puede moderar contenido y restringir la participación ante incumplimientos. Usa las opciones de denuncia de GitHub para reportar abusos.
