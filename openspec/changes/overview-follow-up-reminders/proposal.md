# Overview follow-up reminders

## Why

People can already record interviews and follow-up dates, but they have to open the activity calendar to remember what needs attention. Surface the next actions on the overview so the application tracker is easier to keep current.

## Scope

- Show up to three overdue follow-ups and up to three interviews or non-overdue follow-ups due in the next 30 local calendar days.
- Link each item to its application and provide a link to the full activity timeline.
- Reuse the existing workspace-scoped timeline API and the viewer's time zone.
- Keep this read-only; do not change notification settings or send messages.

## Out of scope

- New notifications, reminders, or background jobs.
- Changing when an interview or follow-up is stored or considered overdue.
- Automatic updates to application stages.
