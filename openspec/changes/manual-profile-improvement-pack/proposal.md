# Proposal

## Why

Issue #51 asks for practical ways to improve a professional profile while ensuring every factual claim remains grounded in approved experience and nothing is sent to LinkedIn.

## What Changes

- Add a manual, copyable profile pack on the profile page.
- Derive headline text and an About draft from approved structured experience or an approved summary; show exact evidence alongside each output.
- Reuse approved experience statements verbatim as copyable bullets.
- Surface existing saved-job skill gaps as review prompts and clearly labeled portfolio ideas, with links to the source jobs.
- Keep all generated text local and include its evidence when copied.

## Out of Scope

- AI or Codex generation, LinkedIn connections, scraping, importing, editing, or publishing.
- Turning a suggested skill or portfolio idea into a profile fact.
- Rewriting or embellishing approved experience statements.
